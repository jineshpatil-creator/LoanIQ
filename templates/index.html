About LoanIQ

LoanIQ is a demonstration of a loan approval classifier. A scikit-learn decision tree, trained on historical loan applications, makes every prediction. Nothing is hardcoded or simulated.

Results reflect patterns in the training data, including its biases and gaps, so treat them as a model output to study, not as a verdict on a person.

Not a lending decision

A LoanIQ result is a model prediction only. It is not credit advice and does not reflect how any lender would actually assess an application.
